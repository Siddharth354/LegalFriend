**One real mistake caught mid-fetch, worth recording**: a search result pointed at `cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/.../2023111188.pdf` claiming to be the NI Act bare act. It downloaded fine (200 OK, valid PDF) but its actual content was an unrelated 235-page university "Principles of Insurance and Banking" course-note PDF — a `.gov.in`-hosted domain does not by itself guarantee the content matches the filename/title. Caught by extracting text and searching for `"Dishonour"` + `"138"`, which returned nothing. Discarded, re-sourced from India Code directly. Lesson: always verify a downloaded legal PDF's actual text content against a known clause, not just that the URL looks official and the download succeeded.
