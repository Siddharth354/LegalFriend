# Sarvam Epoch Buildathon — Submission Q&A

## Project name

Nyaya Dost

## Who are we building for?

A gig worker or small-shop owner in a Tier-2 or Tier-3 town who took a short-term loan from an app-based lender at a punishing daily interest rate, and is now getting called at midnight by a recovery agent threatening jail time if they don't pay. They don't read English legal text, don't know a lawyer, and the moment they need an answer is the moment nothing else is open.

## What are we building?

A voice-in, voice-out legal-aid agent. The person speaks their situation in Hindi or their own language — no forms, no typing, no reading. A multi-agent pipeline (intent classification, fact extraction, retrieval over Indian statutes, drafting, evidence-checking, and a final judge that only releases claims it can actually cite) grounds the answer in the RBI Digital Lending Directions, the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, and the Negotiable Instruments Act, then speaks the answer straight back to them. If the law doesn't cover something, it says so and asks a focused follow-up instead of guessing.

## Why would they use this over what they do today?

Today they either do nothing — scared, unsure if the threat is even legal, unable to afford a lawyer — or they try to search confusing legal text online that assumes they can already read English and already know which Act applies. A helpline, if they even know one exists, is closed at midnight when the threatening call actually comes in. Nyaya Dost is available the instant the threat happens, in the language they're already speaking, and answers with a citation instead of a guess — and it's honest when the excerpts it has don't cover the specific claim (e.g. it won't tell someone an interest rate is illegal unless a supplied excerpt actually sets that limit).
